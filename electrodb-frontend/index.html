JK Flip-Flop

JK Flip-Flop

Flip-Flops Edge-Triggered Sequential Digital

The JK flip-flop is a clocked sequential element. The detail page should identify the input signals, active clock edge or enable condition, asynchronous preset/clear behavior if present, and the output-state convention. Timing-sensitive designs must also respect setup time, hold time, and clock-to-output delay from the relevant datasheet.
